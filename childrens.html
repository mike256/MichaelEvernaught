More Than a Magic Trick

A Journey Into Imagination

When Michael Evernaught steps into the room, the living room, classroom, or auditorium transforms into a starlit theater of curiosity. Rather than asking children to sit quietly and merely watch, Michael invites them into the story—helping magical objects vanish, impossibly appear, and obey their spoken wishes.

Every performance is thoughtfully calibrated to the age group in front of him: gentle, colorful, silly-smart wonder for younger children, and jaw-dropping sleight of hand and interactive mysteries that captivate older kids—and keep parents laughing right alongside them.

Peace of Mind for Parents & Organizers

Stress-Free From Arrival to Applause

You focus on enjoying the smiles and taking photos—Michael handles the entertainment from the moment he arrives.

Zero Mess & Compact Setup

No confetti cannons, live animals, fire, or messy props. Michael needs only a small 6×6 ft performance corner in your living room, backyard shade, or classroom.

Age-Calibrated Routines

A show for 5-year-olds looks completely different from a show for 10-year-olds. Every routine is tailored to match your group’s exact developmental stage and attention span.

Inclusive & Kind-Hearted

Volunteers are always celebrated, never embarrassed. Shy children can participate comfortably from their seats while eager helpers join right up front.

Pacific Northwest Service
