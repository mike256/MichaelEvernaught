Great community entertainment does more than pass the hour—it awakens nostalgia, sparks lively table conversation for days afterward, and makes every resident feel genuinely valued. Michael Evernaught brings the warmth of classic theater directly to your community room or dining hall.

For Activities & Life Enrichment Directors

What Your Residents Will Experience

Every routine is selected for high visual contrast, clear narrative pacing, and comfortable seated sightlines:

  • Highly Visual Everyday Objects: Classic miracles with vibrant silks, silver coins, linking rings, and oversized cards that are effortless to see and follow from across the room.
  • Dignified, Gentle Participation: Warm, respectful audience interaction that celebrates residents as the stars of the show—never putting anyone on the spot.
  • Sensory & Audio Comfort: Clear voice projection (with optional self-contained amplification) and zero startling noises, strobe lights, or messy props.
  • Turnkey Reliability: Prompt arrival, compact 10-minute setup, W-9 vendor readiness, and special community/nonprofit rate tiers.
