About Michael

"You sir, are no muggle!" -Vinny

Michael is a native of the beautiful Columbia River Gorge at the foot of the cascade mountains in the Pacific Northwest. His unique story driven style is accessable to all ages and environments and sure to leave a lasting memory.

A lifetime student of the prestigious McBride Magic and Mystery School in fabulous Las Vegas Nevada. Michaels dedication to the craft and art of magic is highlighted in every performance.

From the smallest intimate gathering to convention floors or the largest stages Michael Evernaught™ Shares classical magic delivered with meaningful stories celebrating inspiration and encouragement.
