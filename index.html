The Artist

Behind the Curtain

“Peer behind the curtain and rediscover a sense of wonder through the art of the impossible. Explore the thin line between reality and mystery as you embrace the curiosity and inspiration found in the extraordinary.”

From the dramatic landscapes of the Pacific Northwest and the Columbia River Gorge to private estates and corporate stages worldwide, Michael Evernaught serves as a bridge between the possible and the inexplicable. His craft is far more than a performance—it is a precision-tuned journey through classical sleight of hand, psychological intrigue, and theatrical storytelling.

Known as the International Man of Wonder, Michael invites discerning audiences to question their own senses in an atmosphere of warmth, wit, and refinement. In a world saturated with easy explanations, he offers your guests the rare luxury of truly being astonished.
